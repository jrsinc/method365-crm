Method365 CRM for Outlook

The Method365 CRM panel shows the people you email as the CRM knows them, and lets you log emails and create records without leaving Outlook.

Reading an email

Click Method365 CRM on the ribbon. The panel shows the sender's contact, lead and account, their opportunities and quotes. You can log the email on a record, or create an opportunity, lead, contact, account, case or follow-up task from it. Pin the panel to keep it open as you move between emails.

Writing an email

Insert a product table from the Product Master, attach a quote as a PDF (a picture of the quote, so its text can't be copied), or insert your CRM signature.

Signing in

The first time, click Sign in to the CRM and sign in with your CRM email and password. The panel stays connected for 30 days. To disconnect, click Sign out in the panel, or end it under Profile → signed-in devices in the CRM.

Help
